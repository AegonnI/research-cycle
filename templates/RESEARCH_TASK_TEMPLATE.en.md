# Working research task

The agent fills this from the user's words; manual placeholders are not required.
Keep only applicable fields. Mark unknowns instead of inventing values.

- Research question and practical purpose.
- Deliverable, audience, language and format.
- Permitted inputs/sources and versions; fixed evaluation inputs if applicable.
- Sufficiency: a supported qualitative answer or a specified metric/target.
- Verification: source support, calculations, controls, repeats or independent evaluation as appropriate.
- Permitted methods, fixed conditions and material assumptions.
- Specified resources/deadlines and stopping rule; no hidden limits for unspecified fields.
- Workspace, read/write boundaries, available tools/runtime and optional manifest.
- Short initial plan and record format.
- Blocking questions, other assumptions and capability limits.

For a skill ablation hold other conditions equal. If comparing entire processes,
state their differences. Do not transfer prior outcomes or held-out data into a clean run.
